How to compare them yourself

  1. Open RiverChat and pick GPT-5.6 Sol as your model.
  2. Click + next to the model picker and add Kimi K3.
  3. Send one prompt. Both answers appear next to each other in the conversation tree.
  4. Continue from the better answer, or branch from both to see where they diverge.

Benchmarks rarely match your own work, so test prompts from your real tasks: a bug you fixed last week, an email you need to write, or a document you want summarized.
